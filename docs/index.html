Which attribution model tells the truth

Ninety days of synthetic ad traffic in which every channel's real effect on conversion is known. A dbt pipeline runs seven attribution models over it, so each model's channel credit can be checked against the answer. Every figure on this page is read from the pipeline's tested marts.

          How the ground truth works

          The generator decides whether each simulated user converts with a logistic model of the channels that reached them, and that model's coefficients are known. The true credit for a channel is its exact Shapley share of the expected lift those coefficients produce, summed over every user. On real data there is no such answer, which is why attribution is argued about. Here it can be measured.
